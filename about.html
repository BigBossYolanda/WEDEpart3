Our Story

Katz Hair-do was founded in 2019 with a mission to make luxury haircare accessible. We specialize in protective styles that celebrate natural beauty.

What started as a small chair in Loop Street has grown into two branches - City Centre and Claremont - serving over 2000 clients across Cape Town.

We believe every client deserves to feel confident. From knotless braids that last 6 weeks to flawless wig installs, we do it with care.

FAQs

► What are your hygiene protocols?

All tools are disinfected with Barbicide after each use. Capes are washed daily. Stations are sanitized between clients. We use disposable gloves for colour and treatment services.

► How long do knotless braids last?

Knotless braids last 4-6 weeks with proper care. We include a free aftercare sheet with every install: wrap at night, oil scalp 2x per week, no heavy products.

► Do you do walk-ins?

Yes, City Centre branch takes walk-ins Mon-Sat 9am-7pm, but booking via Contact or Enquiry is recommended to avoid waiting.
